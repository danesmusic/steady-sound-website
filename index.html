Questions, answered

Does Steady Sound record or save anything?

No. Steady Sound processes sound live and never records or saves your audio. Nothing is written to disk and nothing leaves your Mac.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 14.2 (Sonoma) or later. Steady Sound uses a newer Core Audio feature that isn't available on older versions of macOS.

Does it work with Netflix and other streaming services?

Yes. We've tested it with Netflix in Safari, YouTube in Chrome, the Apple TV app and Plex. It works on your Mac's system audio, so any app that plays sound works.

Does it work with AirPods and other Bluetooth audio?

Yes. Steady Sound follows your Mac's current output, so switching to AirPods or headphones mid-playback keeps working.

Will voices get out of sync with the picture?

No. Steady Sound adds only a few milliseconds of delay, too little to see.

Is there a free trial?

No. At $5 a year we kept it simple: buy it, paste your key, and it works on up to 3 Macs. If something isn't working for you, email us and we'll help.

How do I download it?

Right after checkout you'll get an email with your download link and your license key. Open the app, paste the key, and you're set.

Do I need to be online?

Only to activate. After that it checks your license now and then, and keeps working for up to two weeks without a connection.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes, one license covers up to 3 Macs. To free up a slot, open the app's About window on a Mac you no longer use and click Deactivate this Mac.

What happens if I cancel?

Steady Sound keeps working until the end of the year you paid for. After that it switches off and your Mac's audio goes back to normal. Nothing breaks.

Will it change how my music sounds?

Steady Sound is tuned for movies, TV and video. Music will sound a bit more even. Turn it off with one click from the menu bar when you'd rather hear music untouched.
