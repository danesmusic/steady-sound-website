A few questions

Does Steady Sound record or save anything?

No. Steady Sound never records or saves your audio. It processes sound live and nothing is written to disk.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 14.2 (Sonoma) or later. Steady Sound uses a newer Core Audio feature that isn't available on older versions of macOS.

Does it work with AirPods and other Bluetooth audio?

Yes. Steady Sound follows your Mac's current output device, so switching to AirPods or headphones mid-playback keeps working.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes, one license covers up to 3 Macs. Deactivate a Mac you're no longer using from the app's About window to free up a slot.
